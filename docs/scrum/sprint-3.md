## Changes during the sprint

Requirements changed during the sprint; every change is in the story's own
change log in [`docs/backlog/`](../backlog/README.md). The larger ones:

- **Before planning, the Week 7 customer changes.** Six changes arrived on
  2 Oct, all required for Release 1 ([SCRUM-158](../backlog/SCRUM-158.md)).
  The team decided on 7 Oct to keep Sprint 2 as committed and plan the changes
  into Sprint 3: SCRUM-159 to SCRUM-166 were created that day, one or more per
  change, each naming the Sprint 1 and 2 criteria it supersedes. Also on
  7 Oct, SCRUM-167 was merged into SCRUM-166 (re-estimated 5 to 8) and
  SCRUM-133 into SCRUM-85 (3 to 5).
- **13 Oct, Week 9 clarification:** opening hours apply to the booking period,
  not setup or turnaround (SCRUM-34 AC7, SCRUM-38 AC4 and AC15, SCRUM-159
  AC9); a hold is confirmed only after Venue Staff approve it (SCRUM-163,
  remaining work re-estimated at 3).
- **14 Oct, PO approved:** SCRUM-161's replacement-venue criteria moved to
  SCRUM-38 and SCRUM-162.
- **17 Oct, PO approved:** the change-request case of SCRUM-171 AC5 and
  SCRUM-75 AC11 moved to SCRUM-128 AC11.
- **18 Oct, PO approved:** SCRUM-38's search covers one day.
